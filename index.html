THE PITCH

BRAWL YOUR MATES WITH
SMART PLAYS AND DUMB ITEMS

The win goes to whoever times it right,
not whoever twitches fastest.

THE PROBLEM

Party brawlers are either too twitchy for casuals or too random to feel fair. Nobody's happy.

OUR ANSWER

Depth comes from item timing, not reflexes. Anyone can pick up a pad. The smart player still wins.

The rule we design by: items beat aim, action stays readable, and every round is worth clipping.

THE GAME
AT A GLANCE

ITEM-DRIVEN
PARTY BRAWLER

A fast-round spaceship brawler where the wild stuff comes from your choices, plus a little luck.

Fly through a wraparound arena, grab and fire 30+ modules, and knock your friends out of the sky.

  • 3 module slots so loadouts are a real choice
  • Screen wrap for Asteroids-style spatial play
  • One-hit kills, instant respawns, high stakes
  • Physics-based drift and momentum that feel great
  • Pick up and play with zero setup
